## Defects

1. Station roof hides the rink and the boarding in the wow frame (W-thrill-noon, bottom 40 %). Likely cause: the wow cam sits behind the station; fix by raising the cam / moving it to the loop side. Owner: engine (shots cam) or props (roof size).
2. Siren ring reads as a hoop on the roof (W-thrill-noon). Ring is a cream ring in the station lamp scene (`coasterLampScene`, `QCM.lamp.sirenRing`: 2 rings, r 0.2-0.9, travel 1.2); too small and too opaque for 0.8 scale. Owner: life. Files: `src/render/culture-movers.ts`, `src/content/themes/qa-coaster.ts`.
3. Dark gravel patches are speckled and hard-edged next to the station (D-coaster-site 500-900 x 540-700; W-thrill-noon 1300-1700 x 620-900). Probably a path / apron splat with a noisy mask; they read as a texture error. Owner: worldgen / shader. Files: `src/world/gen/plans/qa-park.ts` (apron / pads), terrain ground-cover shader.
4. D-coaster-loop and D-coaster-lift cams are inside the lattice and out of focus. Owner: engine. File: `src/content/shots.ts` COASTER_SHOTS (cams `9.5,3.8,-4.5,...` and `-11,5,6,...`).
5. D-coaster-loop-in 297 calls / 860k tris, one call from the 300 cap, dev shot unasserted. Likely hangars + station + 5 lifts in frame. Owner: engine (cam) or props (LOD).
6. Low memory 79 / 80 at the ladder peak (both presets); medium W27 175 / 180. Owner: engine (budgets / `measureGpuMemory`).
7. Flat green diamond next to the track in D-coaster-lamp-on: bug-hunt firefly sprite, reads as a glitch at this scale. Owner: life.
8. Ratchet click, nod, boarding pop, brake: not verifiable from 1 s stills. Needs a 0.1-0.25 s step sequence or a real-GPU video capture. Owner: qa (follow-up).
