## Live finding (the one real bug)

A follow-up sent within milliseconds of `done` produced `session error (turns 1, lastSeq 3)` about a second after `running`, while the resumed process was still alive. In `server/bridge.ts` `attachProc` the previous run's `proc.on('close')` handler calls `endRun(run, ...)` on the shared `run` object, not on its own process. `sendSession` has already reset `gotResult` to false and republished `running`, so when the old process closes, `endRun` sees a live session without a result and publishes `error` ("exit 0 without result") and finishes the request as error. Observed consequences: the session read as not live, so a second `send` ("ikinci") was accepted and ran concurrently; both runs completed (answers "Motor." and a clarifying question), `baseTurns` was reset by the second send, so `turns` ended at 2 instead of 3, and the status flipped back to done. A person clicking Gönder a second after done is unlikely to hit it; a fast double click or a scripted client does. Code-read plus one observed run, not reproduced a second time (live budget). Smallest fix: capture `proc` in `attachProc` and ignore a close or error event whose proc is not the current `run.proc`.
