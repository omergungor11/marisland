## 4. Known nits: confirmed or dismissed
**4a. Green smudge at the crag foot: CONFIRMED, matters (medium).** W-meridian-noon: dark olive blurry patch on the crag face above and left of the dial, left of the tree
(~x 800-900, y 490-560 of 1920x1080). W14: soft green washes on the crag toe near the trees (~x 430-560, y 540-620) and on the quad-side face. D-m28-deck and D-m28-sundial show the
same effect larger: the lawn cells under the deck / dial pads blend into the cliff colour as a round soft halo. Probable cause (not verified in code): pads flattened and painted lawn on a
steep crag, the zone blend bleeds up the face; D-107 paints cliff only within 2 u of the stair and 16 u of the summit foot-of-riser cells, not around the deck / dial pads. Owner: worldgen / shader.
**4b. Stair lamp light pool too strong at night: CONFIRMED, matters (medium).** W-star-stair-night: a saturated orange wash covers the flight, the crag face beside it (~x 780-1080, y 330-880)
and the foot, brighter than the dome windows, reads as fire or lava rather than six small teal-glass lanterns. D-m28-stair-night shows the same. The orange hue also differs from the teal lamp gems.
Owner: shader / props (night light radius, intensity, hue). Cheap fix, high return: this is the signature night frame.
**4c. No comet in W-star-stair-night: CONFIRMED, low-medium.** No comet, and no stars in frame, the sky is flat dark blue. Cause as stated by 526 (comet bearing 160 deg vs camera ~75 deg, 22:05 is 2 s into the
ritual), not verified in code. The shot name promises a star moment. Owner: engine (shot camera / simt).
**4d. Sundial noon shadow invisible from wow distance: DISMISS as a bug.** W-meridian-noon: blade is a clear dark triangle, brass line and plates legible, shadow not visible (86 deg sun). Physically right and test-pinned.
**4e. NEW: XII plate reads "IIX".** D-m28-sundial (viewed from the south): the highlighted gold plate at the end of the noon line shows "IIX" and the plate at the lower right looks rotated or mirrored; other plates read
normally. Probably plate numerals rotated 180 deg or mirrored on the sides facing the default view (not verified in code). The XII plate is the dial's focal point and should read XII. Owner: props.
**4f. Big dish at 0.8 scale on 1001: DISMISS.** Visible and readable behind the dome in W14 and at the left in W-meridian-noon.
**4g. Second rim deck hidden behind the crag at noon: CONFIRMED, low.** W-meridian-noon shows no second deck; W14 shows one at the left. Acceptable, D-m28-deck proves the deck itself.
