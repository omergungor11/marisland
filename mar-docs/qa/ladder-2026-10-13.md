## Causes and fixes

1. **The medium / high masks were wrong (measurement defect, capture).** `debug=mask` went through the post chain.
   NEUTRAL tone mapping subtracts 0.04 (linear), so land `#50C878` came out as (57, 194, 107), and water and sand
   no longer matched their classes either. 99 % of each mask classified as "structure" and water counted as solid.
   Every medium / high step compared the whole frame (`landFrac` 1.00), sea included. **Fix:** the mask renders
   straight to the canvas (`app.ts`). Medium / high masks are now exact, like low. This explains the old
   "medium / high miss far more than low" note from 2026-10-09.
2. **L-hq drift at 30 / 21 u (parallax).** Ground-only drift is 0.5 / 0.4. The near frames show more structure
   over the same ground footprint: the share goes from 0.40 to 0.52, because roofs and walls lean over the
   ground in close perspective. The colour itself does not change. **Fix (metric):** `regionDrift` leaves out
   cells whose class (ground vs structure) differs between the two frames, unless they are more than 35 % of
   the region. Same pattern as D-037. A new unit test covers both cases: a leaning roof over 20 % of the region
   is ignored, a structure over 60 % is still flagged. The test fails with the rule off. After the fix:
   30 / 21 u read 2.12 / 1.84 (low).
3. **L-research 30 → 21 (parallax).** The target is the quad fountain. Halls 10–14 u tall and the clock tower
   stand within ~10 u of it, and from 21–30 u the camera sits at their roof line. Their off-plane parallax is
   several cells, more than the ±1 slack. Nothing pops. **Fix (ladder camera):**
   `FRAMING.ladder.shift.research = { right: 20, back: 8 }` moves the target to the low parterre garden,
   and the halls stay in the upper background. 30 → 21 now reads p95 7.0 / 7.7 (low / high). This also cleared
   low's pair 140 blob.
4. **L-pairs-marketing 380 on low (real pop).** The Coding island shows at the frame edge. `solarRow` was
   tier 1, so at T0 the solar fields were grey gravel pads and at 380 u the blue rows popped in.
   **Fix (registration):** `solarRow` → tier 0. W1 +4 calls / +3 k triangles, W11 +2 calls.
5. **L-pairs-design 45 on high (real defect).** At T3, high turns DOF on. Nothing called `setFocus`, so the
   focus fell back to the screen-centre ray ∩ sea plane, which lies 15–25 u past the raised Design campus.
   The whole T3 frame was blurred (pair mean 2.95–3.02). **Fix:** the composer focuses on the orbit target
   every frame (`app.ts`). Pair 45 is now 2.27. `D-pool-ducks` is the only high shot outside the ladder;
   its target is the pool, which is where it should focus.
6. **L-research 340 → 240 (low, real defect, still open).** The Design island at the top-left edge loses its
   forest at T1. The T0 tree blobs are already off, and tier-1 trees dither out over `TIER_FADE[1]` =
   [320, 420] u from the camera, so the far half of a T1 frame shows stipple. The same thing is visible in W11
   (far islands bare). The research shift (cause 3) moves most of the Design island out of the frame, so the
   step passes (0.8 %), but the defect remains.
