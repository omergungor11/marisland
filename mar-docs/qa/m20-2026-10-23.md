### Low — L-devops 30→21 (fixed: framing)

The ladder target is the village anchor, and on 1001 it sits on the cone's cliff (y 19): the plateau is at 7 on one
side and the summit at 29 on the other. From 30 / 21 u the basalt strata off the target plane slide several
cells between steps. This is parallax, not a pop. `FRAMING.ladder.shift.devops = { right: -12, back: 10 }`
moves the target onto the flat harbour plateau (y 7). L-devops and L-pairs-devops now pass on low; on medium the step miss is gone too,
but the moved drift footprint now takes in more of the harbour (containers, halls), and the far T0 frame at 480 u
reads 4.57 (before 3.41): the same far-LOD colour family as Marketing below. Tried and rejected: (−14, −4) put a
cooling tower beside the target (30→21 p95 14.7, blob 3.2 %, low); (−8, 6) drift 700 / 480 4.00 / 4.70 (medium).
